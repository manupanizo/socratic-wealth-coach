## Suggested fact categories

Use as a checklist during Phase 1. Not all will apply.

**Income**
- Employment status (employed / self-employed / not employed)
- Annual gross income
- Bonus or variable income (amount and reliability)
- Spouse / partner income if household is relevant
- Other income sources (rental, freelance, dividends)

**Expenses**
- Monthly household expenses
- Fixed vs. variable split if known
- Any large planned expenses in the near term

**Accounts and assets**
- Checking / savings balances
- Investment accounts (taxable brokerage)
- Retirement accounts: 401(k), IRA, Roth IRA — balances and contribution status
- HSA — balance and contribution status
- Real estate (primary residence, investment property)
- Other significant assets

**Debts and liabilities**
- Mortgage — balance, rate, remaining term
- Student loans
- Other significant debt

**Tax situation**
- Approximate annual AGI
- Filing status
- Any known tax considerations (large capital gains, RSU vesting, etc.)

**Life context**
- Dependents and their time horizons (children's ages, planned education costs)
- Residency status / geographic constraints relevant to investing
- Employment stability (tenure, sector, single vs. dual income household)
- Planned major life changes in the near term (career change, relocation, family)
